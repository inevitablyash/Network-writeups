# Installing Networks & Data Center Infrastructure

---

# MDF — Main Distribution Frame

- Central point of network connectivity in a building  
- Connects:
  - Core switches  
  - Routers  
  - ISP connections  

## Purpose
- Main network distribution area  

---

# IDF — Intermediate Distribution Frame

- Secondary network distribution point  
- Connects end devices and access switches to the MDF  

## Common Location
- Different floors or sections of a building  

---

# Equipment Rack

Used to mount and organize networking equipment.

## Standard Size
- **19-inch width** industry standard  

---

# Rack Units (U)

Rack height measurement.

## Size
`1U = 1.75 inches`

## Common Rack Size
`42U`

---

# Cooling in a Data Center

Cooling prevents overheating of equipment.

## Methods
- Air conditioning  
- Hot aisle / cold aisle design  
- Ventilation systems  

## Importance
- Prevents hardware failure  
- Improves performance and lifespan  

---

# Cable Infrastructure

Organized cabling system for network connectivity.

## Includes
- Copper cabling  
- Fiber optic cabling  
- Patch panels  
- Cable management  

---

# Copper Patch Panel

- Used to organize copper Ethernet cables  

## Common Connector
- RJ45  

---

# Fiber Patch Panel

- Organizes fiber optic cables and connections  

## Benefits
- Easier management  
- Cleaner installation  

---

# Electrical Power Concepts

---

## Ampere (Amp)
- Measures electrical current  

---

## Volt
- Measures electrical potential/voltage  

---

## AC — Alternating Current
- Current changes direction periodically  
- Used in homes and buildings  

---

## DC — Direct Current
- Current flows in one direction  
- Used by most electronic devices internally  

---

# UPS — Uninterruptible Power Supply

Provides backup power during outages.

## Purpose
- Prevents sudden shutdowns  
- Protects equipment  

---

# Types of UPS

## Offline/Standby UPS
- Basic backup power  
- Switches to battery during outage  

---

## Line-Interactive UPS
- Handles small voltage fluctuations  
- Common in businesses  

---

## Online UPS
- Continuous power from battery/inverter  
- Best protection for critical systems  

---

# PDU — Power Distribution Unit

Distributes electrical power to devices in racks.

## Functions
- Multiple power outlets  
- Power monitoring (smart PDUs)  

---

# Environmental Factors

Conditions that affect network equipment.

## Examples
- Temperature  
- Humidity  
- Dust  
- Water leakage  
- Electromagnetic interference (EMI)  

## Importance
- Proper environment improves reliability and hardware lifespan  

---

# Quick Notes

- MDF = Main network room  
- IDF = Secondary network room  
- 1U = 1.75 inches  
- Standard rack width = 19 inches  
- UPS provides backup power  
- PDUs distribute rack power  
- Cooling is critical in data centers
